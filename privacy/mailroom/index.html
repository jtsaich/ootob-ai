mailroom Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026. This policy covers mailroom (formerly cc-statement-sync), a personal tool built and used by Jack Tsai (歐兜拜 ootobai). It is not offered to the public; the only Google account it accesses is its owner's.

What the app does

It runs on its owner's Mac and handles two kinds of email in its owner's Gmail:

  • Credit card statements: extracts the transactions and adds them to a review tab in its owner's own expense spreadsheet.
  • Newsletters its owner subscribes to: sorts them into reading labels, splits each issue into stories, and every morning recommends a few of them to its owner; it also writes a weekly investing digest into its owner's own notes folder. The owner's taps on the recommendations tune future ones.

Google data it accesses, and why

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): reads only bank statement emails and email from a list of newsletter senders its owner chose; everything else is never opened. For those newsletter emails it also checks whether the owner has opened them, as a signal of interest. It moves statement emails between three labels (帳單/待處理, 帳單/已處理, 帳單/失敗) and gives newsletters a reading label (閱讀/…) while taking them out of the inbox. When the owner asks to read a recommended item in full, it puts that newsletter back in the inbox with the label 閱讀/想讀 (and takes it out again if the owner changes their mind). It does not send, delete, or forward email.
  • Gmail settings (gmail.settings.basic): creates the filters that label incoming statement and newsletter emails. It does not change any other setting.
  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets): reads the existing transactions in one spreadsheet to name, categorize, and de-duplicate new rows, and appends rows to that spreadsheet's review tab. It does not modify or delete existing rows.
  • Cloud Pub/Sub (pubsub): receives Gmail's "mailbox changed" notifications so emails are processed when they arrive. The notifications contain no email content.

Where the data goes

  • Processing happens on its owner's computer. Statement PDFs are decrypted in memory and not saved.
  • Extracted transactions are written only to its owner's Google Sheet.
  • Newsletter summaries, the daily recommendations, the owner's feedback on them (which items the owner asked to read in full, weekly "which would you rather have read?" answers, and optional short replies), and which of the selected newsletter emails were opened are kept in a local database on its owner's computer. Recommendation and digest notes are written into its owner's own notes folder on the same computer.
  • Short messages (statement totals with a link to the sheet; the daily recommendations; the weekly digest's highlights and a short learning report) are sent to its owner's own Telegram and LINE accounts. Telegram also delivers the owner's taps and replies back to the app.
  • To summarize, the text of each selected newsletter email is sent to Anthropic's Claude; to rank the recommendations, the stories' summaries are sent together with the owner's written reading preferences. For statements, only merchant names the app doesn't recognize, with the item names and categories already used in the sheet, are sent; amounts, dates, and account details are not. Anthropic's handling is governed by its privacy policy.
  • To download a statement from a bank's website, the app sends that bank the login details its owner configured.

The app has no servers. Google OAuth tokens and other credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain on its owner's computer, alongside a local log and the local database described above.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or shown to anyone other than the owner. Nothing is shared beyond the services listed above.

Google API Services User Data Policy

mailroom's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting its Keychain items (service mailroom) and its folder ~/Library/Application Support/mailroom removes every credential, summary, and local record it kept.
  • Rows it added to the spreadsheet, digest notes it wrote, and labels and filters it created in Gmail stay in the owner's account until the owner deletes them.
